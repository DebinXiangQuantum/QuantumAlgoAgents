# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a community where people can participate safely, equitably, and in good faith. We respect each person's dignity and contribution regardless of background, identity, ability, experience, education, language, philosophy, religion, social position, or other status.

## Expected conduct

Community members should:

- engage honestly, kindly, and with respect for different viewpoints;
- focus criticism on ideas, evidence, code, and reproducible behavior;
- give and receive constructive feedback without humiliation;
- credit sources and other people's work;
- protect confidential and personal information;
- accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and help repair harm;
- present scientific uncertainty and negative evidence responsibly;
- support the purpose and long-term health of the project.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces or while officially representing the project:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or repeated unwanted attention;
- insults, character attacks, demeaning language, or discriminatory stereotyping;
- sexualized conduct or imagery inappropriate to the project context;
- publishing private information without clear permission;
- impersonation or evasion of moderation actions;
- plagiarism, deliberate source erasure, or knowingly fabricated evidence;
- dangerous instructions or exploit details shared outside responsible disclosure;
- disruptive promotion, spam, or communication unrelated to the community's purpose;
- retaliation against a reporter, witness, reviewer, or moderator.

Technical disagreement, rejected claims, and requests for stronger evidence are not harassment when expressed professionally and applied consistently.

## Reporting

Report a possible violation privately to a current Community Moderator using the contact method published on that maintainer's contributor profile. Do not place sensitive incident details in a public issue or discussion. If no private maintainer channel is available, use GitHub's platform abuse-reporting mechanism to request confidential assistance reaching the repository owner.

Moderators will prioritize the safety and confidentiality of reporters, affected people, and witnesses. They may review relevant messages, logs, or other evidence and will disclose information only as needed for a fair response or when legally required.

## Response and repair

Moderators may choose a response proportionate to impact, context, history, and the needs of affected people. Responses can include:

1. a private clarification or warning and a request to correct the behavior;
2. limits on specific activities or contact for a defined period;
3. temporary suspension from project spaces;
4. permanent removal when serious or repeated behavior makes safe participation impossible.

Where appropriate, repair may include acknowledging impact, correcting the record, restoring attribution, apologizing, removing harmful material, or changing project processes. Moderators may skip lower steps for severe incidents. Enforcement decisions are made privately, although an anonymized community notice may be published when it improves safety or transparency.

## Scope

This code applies to repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, chats, events, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone acts as an appointed representative of the project or uses an official project identity.
