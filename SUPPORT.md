# Support

Quantum Algorithm Designer is a research prototype maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no guaranteed response time, consulting service, or compatibility service-level agreement.

## Where to ask

- Reproducible software defects: use the bug report issue form.
- Proposed capabilities or workflows: use the feature request form.
- Correctness, complexity, or advantage concerns: use the scientific claim review form.
- Contribution questions: read `CONTRIBUTING.md`, then open a focused discussion or issue.
- Security vulnerabilities: follow `SECURITY.md` and do not report publicly.
- Community conduct incidents: follow `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` and use a private channel.

Include the plugin version, Python version, operating environment, smallest reproducing input, exact command or MCP call, and sanitized output. Remove secrets and private research data.

## Scope limits

Maintainers may not be able to provide:

- custom theorem proving or independent peer review;
- access to quantum hardware or proprietary simulators;
- guarantees that a candidate is novel or advantageous;
- support for unpublished external backends;
- recovery of unshared models, logs, or experiment artifacts.

Scientific conclusions remain the responsibility of the people making and reviewing the claim.
